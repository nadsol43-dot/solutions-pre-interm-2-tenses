📖 Grammar help — present simple vs present continuous

⏱ Present simple

  • Form: I / you / we / they play · he / she / it plays
  • Use: habits and routines, facts, states
  • Signals: every Saturday, always, usually, on Fridays
  • State verbs (like, want, know, need) are normally simple, not continuous
She plays tennis on Fridays.

💬 Present continuous

  • Form: am / is / are + verb-ing
  • Use: actions happening now or around now
  • Signals: now, right now, today, at the moment, Look!, Listen!
He’s playing football right now.
